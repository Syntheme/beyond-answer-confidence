  • The score is the AUROC: the chance that, for a random pair with one case from each group, the question ranks them the right way round (ties count half). It measures how well two groups are told apart, not the chance that a particular answer is wrong, and we did not test whether an 80% "yes" means an 80% chance that Jev knows. The text classifier looks only at letter patterns. The realistic names join a real first name to a real surname from two different obscure people (600 names). On the same questions, "Do you know?" minus answer confidence was −0.02 (95% interval −0.05 to +0.01) for realistic names and +0.02 (−0.01 to +0.06) without dates.
